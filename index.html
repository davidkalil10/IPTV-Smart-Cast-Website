Community Experience

How to Watch Together

Share the excitement with friends and family, no matter where they are. Follow these simple steps to start your private watch party.

STEP 1

Host a Party

Select any content (Movie, Series, or Live TV). Click the Social Button in the player to create a room and generate your unique Room ID.

STEP 2

Join the Fun

On your friends' devices, tap the "Watch Together" button on the Home Screen. Enter the Room ID provided by the host to connect.

STEP 3

Perfect Sync

The Host controls the remote! Play, pause, and seek actions are instantly synchronized across all devices so no one misses a moment.

Server Requirement

To ensure correct content synchronization, all participating accounts must belong to the same IPTV server.

Pro Tip: Voice Chat New

Want to talk freely? Set up your Agora ID in the app Settings before hosting. This activates real-time voice chat for the whole room!
